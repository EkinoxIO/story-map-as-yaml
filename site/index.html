A visual story map editor whose source of truth is a plain YAML file: easy for AI agents to read and change, easy for people to see and shape.

A second wave, and an old risk

Spec-Driven Development (SDD) is the second wave of AI tooling for building software. It shifts work left: product owners take part and are empowered, and the whole flow from idea to delivery improves, not only the developers' share of it.

The risk is to repeat old mistakes: long specification documents that nobody really reads, or understands.

The story map

Agile workshops were invented to keep the conversation flowing about the direction to take, the work to do and the steps to get there. The story map is one of them: the user's journey laid out as activities and tasks, sliced into releases that each have a goal.

Text for the agents, a picture for the people

Story Map as YAML keeps the story map as a YAML file, so an AI agent can read and update it like any other file in the repository. On top of it, a visual editor keeps people from getting lost in the text: they keep the clarity of the visual map while the text stays the single source of truth.

  • Open your story map file, start from a sample, or start blank.
  • Edit releases, activities, tasks and stories; fold them; reorder them by drag and drop or from the keyboard.
  • In Chrome and Edge, every change is saved straight into your file, comments and formatting included. Your files stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.
  • The format is open: download its JSON Schema and the sample story map.
